Privacy

What stays in this browser

The browser encrypts each packet with AES-GCM before saving it in IndexedDB. Your passphrase stays in memory while the packet is open.

Closeout Kit has no product account or analytics. The sample uses a separate demo:closeout-kit-v1 database that resets when opened.

What travels over the network

The app, generated harbor artwork, and updates load from this product’s origin. Packet content and passphrases are not sent to a server.

Links you add open the original services you chose. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Exports and receipts

Client packet exports are readable HTML files. Encrypted JSON backups require the packet passphrase.

A client acknowledgement form creates a JSON receipt. It contains the packet ID, project name, confirmations, typed name, role, and date.

Delete your data

Use “Delete packet” in the Export stage, or clear this site’s browser data. Either action removes local packets from that browser.

Contact

Send privacy questions to privacy@sociobot.in.
